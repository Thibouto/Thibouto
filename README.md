### 📊 Featured Projects

**[Global Sustainable Energy Dashboard](https://github.com/Thibouto/sustainable-energy-dashboard)** (Tableau Public)
Interactive dashboard on global sustainable energy indicators (176 countries, 2000-2019): electricity access, energy mix, renewable share growth, and CO2 emissions vs GDP per capita.

**[Quantium Retail Analytics](https://github.com/Thibouto/Quantium-retail-analytics)** (Forage job simulation)
Customer segmentation and store layout trial analysis for a retail chips category, with Python and R. Identified the highest-value customer segment and showed with statistical testing that the trial's sales uplift was sustained in only one of the three test stores. Both analyses rebuilt in SQL (BigQuery) with CTEs, window functions and self-joins.

**[French Real Estate Market Analysis](https://github.com/Thibouto/real-estate-france)**
Analyzed 9.1M+ property transactions (2014-2024) with SQL and Python. Uncovered a time-confounded correlation between interest rates and prices, hidden in the naive data.

**[BMW Group Global Sales Analysis](https://github.com/Thibouto/bmw-sales-global)**
Cleaned a deliberately corrupted dataset and analyzed BMW's global sales (2018-2025) across 4 regions. Found +27.5% sales growth and an uneven EV transition, with China leading.
